# Evaluation Note

## Purpose

This note explains why the manuscript evaluates the Security-by-Transformation framework as an **early-stage design-oriented artifact** rather than as a universally validated empirical model.

## Evaluation Posture

The manuscript adopts a design-oriented evaluation stance grounded in design science research. Under this posture, the immediate evidentiary burden is to show that the artifact:

1. is internally coherent,
2. discriminates meaningfully across SME conditions,
3. remains decision-support relevant for prioritization, and
4. stays analytically plausible under resource constraint.

This means the paper is evaluating whether the framework is a defensible and transparent **decision heuristic**, not whether it has already been universally confirmed across all SME contexts.

## Why This Posture Fits the Study

The artifact is built from a bounded literature-grounded construction process rather than from a large primary empirical validation campaign. At this stage, the contribution is the explicit construction logic:

- clearly stated inputs,
- a bounded capability taxonomy,
- an explicit sequencing rationale, and
- transparent claim boundaries.

That posture is consistent with early-stage framework work in design science traditions, where disciplined construction, transparency, and bounded applicability can be evaluated before later rounds of rubric development, expert review, case mapping, or broader field testing.

## Explicit Non-Claims

The package supports the manuscript's explicit restraint on overclaiming. The paper does **not** claim that:

- all SMEs should follow one identical capability pathway,
- the framework replaces sector-specific standards or detailed technical control engineering,
- the framework is already a universally validated maturity model, or
- the current artifact can be procedurally replicated as a fully parameterized scoring instrument without additional rubric development.

## Reproducibility Honesty

The framework is reproducible as a **conceptual construction** with explicit inputs, domains, and sequencing logic. However, the package also makes clear that the current study is not yet presenting a fully parameterized operational instrument. This distinction is central to the manuscript's rigor because it preserves traceability without overstating maturity.
