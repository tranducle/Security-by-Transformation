# Brainstorm Workflow (SOP_IDEATION_SESSION)

**Use when:** Generating and exploring new research ideas.

---

## Step 1: Problem Framing

Define the challenge:

- What domain/area?
- What constraints?
- What's the goal?

---

## Step 2: Idea Generation

### Brainstorming Techniques

- **Free association:** Write everything that comes to mind
- **SCAMPER:** Substitute, Combine, Adapt, Modify, Put to other uses, Eliminate, Reverse
- **Analogies:** How do other fields solve similar problems?
- **Reversal:** What if we did the opposite?

---

## Step 3: Idea Expansion

For promising ideas, explore variations:

- Different scales
- Different contexts
- Different methods
- Combinations of ideas

---

## Step 4: Feasibility Evaluation

Rate each idea:

| Idea | Novelty | Feasibility | Impact | Score |
|------|---------|-------------|--------|-------|
| Idea 1 | 4 | 3 | 5 | 12 |
| Idea 2 | 5 | 2 | 4 | 11 |

---

## Step 5: Gap Identification

For top ideas:

- What's missing to make this work?
- What resources needed?
- What knowledge gaps exist?

---

## Step 6: Prioritization

Create prioritized list:

1. **Quick wins:** High feasibility, good impact
2. **Big bets:** High impact, needs more work
3. **Explorations:** Interesting but uncertain

---

## Step 7: Next Steps

For top 2-3 ideas:

- Immediate actions
- Research needed
- People to consult
