# Paper Hardening Workflow

This workflow triggers `SOP_PAPER_HARDENING` — a 10-agent pipeline that systematically strengthens a paper before submission.

## Trigger Keywords
Say any of these to auto-activate: "harden paper", "strengthen paper", "prepare for review", "bulletproof paper", "pre-submission check"

## Pipeline Agents (Sequential)

### Phase 1: Identity & Positioning
1. **PaperIdentityArchitect** — Define paper type, contribution identity, and positioning strategy
2. **NoveltyDeltaCartographer** — Map novelty delta vs existing work, find gaps and overlaps

### Phase 2: Logic & Evidence
3. **HiddenAssumptionAssassin** — Expose unstated assumptions that weaken arguments
4. **MinimalDecisiveExperimentDesigner** — Identify the minimal experiments that prove core claims
5. **ClaimEvidenceLedger** — Match every claim to its evidence, flag overclaiming

### Phase 3: Robustness
6. **GeneralizationBreaker** — Find boundary conditions where the method fails
7. **NegativeResultsAlchemist** — Transform negative results into insight and limitations

### Phase 4: Reproducibility & Defense
8. **ReproducibilityForensicsInvestigator** — Audit reproducibility gaps
9. **ReviewerWarGamer** — Simulate reviewer attacks, prepare defenses
10. **CrossDisciplinaryImportSmuggler** — Find cross-field insights to strengthen contribution

## Extended Hardening (Optional Add-ons)

After the SOP pipeline, consider running these complementary agents individually:

- **CitationVulnerabilityScanner** — Check citation-claim mismatches
- **StatisticalSanityProsecutor** — Audit statistical methods
- **MethodologicalConfounderHunter** — Find hidden confounders
- **BaselineFairnessAuditor** — Check baseline comparison fairness
- **FigureArgumentArchitect** — Design argumentative figures
- **TerminologyDriftDetector** — Fix terminology inconsistency
- **ScopeCreepGuillotine** — Cut unfocused content
- **CounterfactualReframer** — Generate counterfactual tests
- **ScholarlyToneEqualizer** — Calibrate scholarly tone
- **SubmissionPersonaSimulator** — Simulate reader personas
- **TableCompressionEngine** — Consolidate tables
- **EquationReadabilityInspector** — Audit equation necessity
- **AppendixValueSorter** — Optimize main vs appendix
