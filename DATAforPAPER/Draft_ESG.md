**1. Introduction**

***1.1. Background and motivation***

* Introduction to Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) factors in corporate strategy.
* Importance of the Cost of Debt (CoD) for listed firms' financial health and investment decisions.
* Growing academic and practical interest in the link between sustainable practices (ESG) and financial outcomes.
* Brief overview of theoretical perspectives linking ESG to the cost of debt (e.g., risk reduction, stakeholder theory, agency theory).

***1.2. Problem statement***

* Existence of numerous empirical studies examining the ESG-Cost of Debt nexus, but with potentially conflicting or inconsistent findings.
* Lack of a comprehensive, systematic, bibliometric, and meta-analytic review specifically focused on listed firms to synthesize these findings quantitatively and qualitatively.
* Need to understand the intellectual structure, key trends, and overall effect size of this relationship.

***1.3. Research goals and questions***

Research Goals:

* To systematically identify, synthesize, and categorize existing empirical literature examining the relationship between ESG performance and the cost of debt specifically within listed firms.
* To conduct a comprehensive bibliometric analysis of the identified literature to map the intellectual structure, key contributors, methodological trends, and thematic evolution of research on ESG performance and the cost of debt.
* To quantitatively synthesize the findings from eligible empirical studies using meta-analysis to determine the overall magnitude and direction of the nexus between ESG performance and the cost of debt in listed firms.
* To identify and evaluate potential moderating factors (e.g., specific ESG dimensions, firm characteristics, geographical context, and methodological approaches) that influence the observed relationship between ESG performance and the cost of debt.
* To identify critical research gaps, reconcile conflicting findings, and propose promising avenues for future theoretical and empirical research on ESG performance and the cost of debt based on the comprehensive review and synthesis.

Research Questions:

* RQ1: What is the scope, nature, and characteristics of existing empirical literature examining the relationship between ESG performance and the cost of debt specifically within listed firms?
* RQ2: Which key authors, institutions, and journals are most influential, and what are the predominant methodological trends and thematic evolutions observed in the literature on ESG performance and the cost of debt in listed firms?
* RQ3: What is the overall magnitude and direction of the relationship between ESG performance and the cost of debt in listed firms, as quantitatively synthesized through meta-analysis of eligible empirical studies?
* RQ4: What potential moderating factors (e.g., specific ESG dimensions, firm characteristics, geographical context, or methodological approaches) influence the observed relationship between ESG performance and the cost of debt in listed firms?
* RQ5: Based on the comprehensive review and synthesis, what critical research gaps, unresolved inconsistencies, and promising avenues exist for future theoretical and empirical research on the nexus between ESG performance and the cost of debt in listed firms?

***1.4. Scope and delimitations***

* Focus exclusively on the relationship between ESG performance and the cost of debt.
* Limited to empirical studies involving listed firms (publicly traded companies).
* Excludes conceptual papers, case studies not involving quantitative analysis, and studies on non-listed entities.
